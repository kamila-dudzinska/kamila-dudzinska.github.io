About me

For over eight years I have worked where procurement meets data – at Genpact, Bayer and Carlsberg Shared Services. I prepared regular and ad-hoc reports for the German, Swiss and Swedish markets, reconciled data between SAP Ariba and SAP MM, and cleaned vendor master data.

The part of my work I enjoyed most was always the data: finding the error nobody noticed, turning a long manual task into one click. My Double Click Report, built with SAP Ariba exports and Excel macros, cut GR report preparation time by about 60% for my team.

That's why I taught myself Python (pandas), SQL and Power BI. I build my projects from A to Z, because I want to understand the logic, not just get a result. Today I'm looking for a remote analytical role where deep procurement knowledge and data skills work together.

Lately I've been fascinated by AI – building and working with agents, streamlining processes and finding patterns hidden in data. I love making the most of the potential that's already there, in data, tools and people. I'm ambitious, very well organized, and I genuinely enjoy learning.
